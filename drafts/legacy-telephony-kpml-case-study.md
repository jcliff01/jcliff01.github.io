# Legacy Telephony Modernization: A Seven-Figure Engineering Solution

**Engineering Case Study — Draft, not approved for publication**  
**Jake Hilber | Principal Engineer**

## The Challenge

A major satellite television provider was working to reduce costs associated with legacy telephone infrastructure while preserving interoperability with a newer SIP-based platform. A key technical requirement was maintaining keypad signaling between the legacy environment and the modern platform.

## My Technical Contribution

I identified an opportunity to adapt existing Cisco phone capabilities using SIP Key Press Markup Language (KPML).

By applying an existing capability in a creative way, I developed a logical workaround that enabled keypad information to pass between the legacy telephone system and the newer SIP-based platform. The solution addressed an interoperability constraint without claiming to invent a new protocol.

## Business Impact

The workaround helped overcome a technical obstacle in the customer's modernization effort. My engineering contribution helped my company secure a **seven-figure enterprise support contract**.

## Engineering Significance

This engagement illustrates protocol-level understanding of Cisco telephony and SIP signaling, creative adaptation of existing capabilities, systems-level troubleshooting, and the ability to connect technical problem-solving to business outcomes.

## Broader Lesson

Innovation does not always require inventing a new technology. Sometimes it comes from understanding existing systems deeply enough to see possibilities others have overlooked.
